# Insurance Risk & Claims Analysis
## Project Overview
This project analyses insurance customer data to identify patterns in claim behaviour and risk exposure. The dashboard helps understand how demographic factors, vehicle characteristics, and policy attributes influence insurance claims.

The goal of this project is to demonstrate data analysis and visualization skills by transforming raw insurance data into meaningful business insights using Power BI.

## Business Objective
Insurance companies need to identify high-risk customers and understand factors affecting claim frequency and claim amount. This dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor key metrics and evaluate how customer profile and vehicle details impact insurance risk.

The dashboard supports decision-making in areas such as:
- risk assessment, pricing strategy, customer segmentation, claim management, policy planning
## Dataset Used
- <a href="https://github.com/ManasaraniB/Insurance-Risk-Claims-Analysis/blob/main/insurance_policies_data.xlsx"> Dataset </a>

The dataset contains customer-level insurance policy information including demographic details, vehicle attributes and claim data.

Key fields used in analysis:

- customer demographics (age, gender, marital status, education)
- vehicle details (car make, car year, car use)
- geographic classification (coverage zone)
- claim amount and claim frequency
- household income, number of young drivers in househol

## KPIs Implemented

The following key performance indicators are used to measure insurance performance:

1. Total Policies – total number of policyholders in dataset
2. Total Claim Amount – total financial impact of claims
3. Average Claim Frequency – average number of claims per customer
4. Average Claim Amount – average value of claim per customer
5. Total Male Customers – number of male policyholders
6. Total Female Customers – number of female policyholders
7. Claim Amount by Education & Marital Status – identifies high-risk demographic segments
8. Policy Distribution Metrics – segmentation by car use, coverage zone, age group and education

## Key Insights
- Customers aged 41–60 hold the highest number of policies, showing strong presence of financially stable customers.
- Private car usage dominates the portfolio, indicating lower commercial exposure.
- Urban and suburban zones contribute a large share of policies, highlighting geographic risk concentration.
- Education and marital status show variation in claim amount distribution.
- Households with young drivers may indicate higher risk exposure.
 Policy distribution is concentrated among popular car brands such as Ford and Toyota.
- Male and female policyholders are almost equally distributed.
## Recommendations
- Implement risk-based pricing using age group, car use, and coverage zone.
- Monitor policies with young drivers for higher claim probability.
- Use demographic insights for targeted marketing strategies.
- Focus on high-volume car brands for improved risk assessment.
